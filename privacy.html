2. What information we collect

When you submit a project inquiry, we may collect the information you choose to provide, including your name, organisation or professional affiliation, email address, telephone or WeChat contact, project category, and the content of your inquiry.

Our hosting and form-processing providers may also process limited technical information associated with website access or submission, such as IP address, browser or device information, request timestamps, and security-related logs.

3. Please do not submit sensitive or highly confidential information

The initial inquiry form is intended only for high-level project information. Please do not submit passwords, payment-card data, patient data, health or genetic data, government identifiers, legally privileged material, trade secrets, unpublished patent-enabling details, or other information that requires heightened confidentiality or security. If a project proceeds, appropriate confidentiality and data-sharing arrangements can be established separately.

4. Why we use your information and our legal bases

We process inquiry information to receive, assess and respond to business and project inquiries; to consider potential collaboration; to maintain necessary business correspondence; to protect the security and integrity of our website; and to comply with applicable legal obligations.

Depending on the circumstances, the legal basis may include steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, our legitimate interests in responding to genuine business inquiries and evaluating potential projects, and compliance with legal obligations. Where processing is based on legitimate interests, we consider whether those interests are necessary and proportionate and whether your rights and interests override them.

We do not use submission of this form as consent for unrelated marketing. If we later offer optional marketing communications, any consent request will be separate and voluntary.

5. Service providers and recipients

We use third-party service providers only where reasonably necessary to operate the site and respond to inquiries. At launch, these may include:

  • GitHub Pages / GitHub — website hosting. GitHub states that IP addresses of visitors to GitHub Pages sites are logged and stored for security purposes.
  • Formspree — form submission processing and delivery. Formspree states that it acts as a data processor and relies on Standard Contractual Clauses for relevant international transfers.
  • Email providers — if inquiry notifications are delivered to our business email account.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect legal rights, or to professional advisers where necessary and subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations.

6. International transfers

Because our service providers or project activities may involve more than one country, personal data may be processed outside your country of residence, including outside the EEA. Where applicable law requires safeguards for an international transfer, we will rely on an appropriate mechanism such as an adequacy decision, Standard Contractual Clauses, or another lawful transfer mechanism.

7. Retention

As a default internal policy, we aim to retain inactive or unsuccessful project inquiries for no longer than 12 months after the last substantive interaction, unless a longer period is reasonably necessary for legal claims, compliance, security, or another lawful purpose. If an inquiry develops into a client, partner or project relationship, relevant records may be retained under the retention rules applicable to that relationship.
