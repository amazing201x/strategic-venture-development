Cookie Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026
Current configuration: this site is intentionally designed without advertising, behavioural tracking, analytics pixels, embedded third-party media, or remotely loaded web fonts. As a result, HESYN does not currently deploy a non-essential cookie consent banner.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small files or identifiers that a website or related service may store on, or access from, a visitor’s device. Similar technologies can perform comparable functions.

2. What HESYN currently uses

HESYN does not intentionally set non-essential cookies for advertising, behavioural profiling, cross-site tracking, or audience analytics on this website.

Our own site code does not use cookies, localStorage, sessionStorage, advertising pixels, Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, LinkedIn Insight Tag, or remotely hosted font services.

3. Strictly necessary and provider-side technical processing

Infrastructure providers may process technical information necessary for security, network delivery, abuse prevention, and operation of their services. For example, GitHub states that IP addresses of visitors to GitHub Pages sites are logged and stored for security purposes.

The project inquiry form is processed by Formspree when you choose to submit it. Merely viewing our webpage does not cause our locally hosted form JavaScript to contact a third-party JavaScript CDN.

4. Why there is no “Accept All” banner

Under European cookie rules, user consent is generally required before non-essential cookies are stored or accessed; technically necessary cookies are treated differently. Because our current design does not intentionally deploy non-essential cookies, we do not present an “Accept / Reject” consent banner.

5. If we add analytics or marketing technologies later

If HESYN later introduces analytics, advertising, remarketing, embedded third-party media, or another technology that requires prior consent, we will update this policy and implement an appropriate consent mechanism before activating those technologies for users where consent is required.
